# 11 AI Skills Promised to Run My Channel. Here's the Truth

Formula: The Teardown + I Tried It. Every figure here comes from our own tests of the repo.

## HOOK (0:00-0:15)
A reel told me 11 AI skills could run my YouTube channel. I tested every one for 7 days.

## TURN (0:15-0:45)

[ON SCREEN: the repo's folder tree, 11 skills highlighted]

## BEAT 1 - What's actually inside
First surprise: there are no agents. It's 11 instruction files and 6 small Python scripts, about
1,500 lines in total. Nothing logs in to YouTube. Nothing uploads. You do that part.
[ON SCREEN: line count, "uploads: 0"]

## BEAT 2 - The hook scorer
The scorer that's supposed to pick your hook counts words like "you", "never" and "why". I gave it
pure clickbait and it scored 77, "strong". A real hook scored 39. So I fixed it: now the clickbait
scores 40.
[ON SCREEN: 77 vs 39, then 40 vs 39 after the fix]

## BEAT 3 - What actually works
The outlier finder is genuinely good. It ranks videos against their own channel, not by raw views,
so a small channel's hit shows up. The retention reader tells you the exact second people left and
what you were saying. And the chapter tool refuses to make chapters YouTube won't show.
[ON SCREEN: outlier list at 5x, retention cliff at 24 seconds with the transcript line]

## PAYOFF
So, the truth: it doesn't run your channel. It's a good checklist with three useful tools. Keep the
outlier finder, the retention reader and the title checker. Treat the hook score as a warning
light, not a judge.
[ON SCREEN: keep / warning-light / skip cards]
