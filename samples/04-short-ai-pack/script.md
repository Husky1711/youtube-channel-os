# Short: AI Scored Clickbait 77. My Hook Got 39.

Vertical, 35-45 seconds. Loop point: the last line sets up the first.

## HOOK (0:00-0:03)
An AI tool rated clickbait 77 out of 100. My real hook got 39.
[ON SCREEN: "77 vs 39" - different words from the spoken line]

## BODY
Here's the clickbait: "You will never believe why you lose 3 hours before you quit, but nobody
knows how." It means nothing. But it says "you", "never" and "why", and that's what the tool
counts. So I changed it. Each trigger word now counts twice at most, and a line that's mostly
trigger words loses points.
[ON SCREEN: trigger words highlighted in yellow, then a "max 2" counter]

## PAYOFF
The clickbait now scores 40. Still not great at spotting good hooks, but it can't be gamed any more.
[ON SCREEN: 77 crossed out, 40 written in]

## LOOP LINE
So next time a tool scores your hook, ask what it's counting. Because this one gave clickbait a 77.
[ON SCREEN: back to "77 vs 39"]
